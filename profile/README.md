## 🏛️ About the Lab

**Scholar Saver** is a responsible agentic AI platform and engineering lab dedicated to student financial sovereignty. We don't just build apps; we build **deterministic AI infrastructure** that helps students manage money, claim grants, and stay in education.

Recognized as the **UK Digital Startup of the Year 2025**, our mission is to scale behavioral AI nudging to millions of students across the UK and beyond.

📍 **London, UK** &nbsp;·&nbsp; **FinTech North** · **Startupbootcamp** · **Tech Nation Libra**
